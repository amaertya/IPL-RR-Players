Engineering a Winning Pressure Evaluation Framework

In elite T20 cricket, standard averages and strike rates hide how players perform when the match is on the line. Our analytical architecture defines pressure dynamically across three core match-context signals:

01

High Required Run Rate

In second-innings run chases when RRR exceeds 8.50 runs/over, placing immediate scoreboard pressure on batters.

02

Death Overs Phase

Deliveries in Overs 16 through 20 across any innings, testing maximum boundary acceleration and containment.

03

Structural Collapses

Situations where 4+ wickets have fallen within the first 15 overs, demanding rebuilding resilience.
